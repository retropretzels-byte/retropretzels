I grew up in the Philly suburbs eating countless pretzels. At school, baseball games, wherever. A salty, chewy, delicious soft pretzel was a given. When I moved to Philly and didn't have much money, I practically lived off food cart pretzels with no complaints. It's a Philly thing. You don't even think about it.

Then I moved to Ventura. I love it here but…no Philly pretzels. That was a problem. So now I'm making them. I want my friends and neighbors to have pretzels for family events, their kids' soccer games, the perfect complement to a local craft beer, or just cause they're in the mood.

Retro Pretzels are made from simple, high-quality ingredients. Flour, water, salt, and yeast. The trick isn't what they're made of, but how they're made. But don't worry about that. Just enjoy.
